      HONEST ABOUT THE LIMITS

      Active matchup to a knockout.

      The bounded forecast searches hit chance, damage rolls, turn order, sleep counters, freeze, paralysis and confusion toward a knockout. Sleep counters expire inside each path; unfinished paths produce bounds instead of an HP-based guess. Both sides choose simultaneously through a mixed-strategy minimax model; this is not verified Run & Bun AI. Move secondary effects, supported status/setup/recovery moves, burn and poison residual damage, and selected item effects are modeled. Multi-hit attacks roll damage and critical hits independently per hit; berries and Focus Sash resolve between hits. Recoil, survival/contact triggers, Protect/Substitute, supported conditional and multi-turn attacks, weather damage and effect expiry also change future states. Missing live counters remain unresolved. Switching, full teams, double battles, unsupported callbacks and unverified ROM timing remain outside the supported scope.

      Reads both active Pokémon, exact HP/stats, moves/PP, Accuracy/Evasion stages, status, items, abilities, weather, terrains, screens, Tailwind, Trick Room, Taunt, Disable and Encore. Choice locks and other temporary effects remain unverified. Only the verified Run & Bun 1.07 ROM fingerprint is accepted. An unsupported ROM, unverified weather state or stale connection clears the bar.
